Ring up a cut

Barber POS is a fast, offline-first point of sale built for barber shops. Tap the services, choose Cash or Whish, print the receipt, and see your daily and monthly totals at a glance.

FEATURES

Everything a barber counter needs.

No accounts, no internet required to take payments. Your data lives on the device and can be backed up whenever you like.

⚡

One-tap sales

Service tiles in a responsive grid. Tap to add or remove, and the running total updates instantly.

💵

Cash & Whish

Record how each customer paid. Totals are split by payment method in every report.

🧾

Receipts

A clean receipt screen after each sale, with one-tap print or save as a compact A6 PDF.

✂

Service manager

Add, edit and price services. Switch a service off to hide it from the sales screen without losing history.

📊

Live reports

Daily and monthly views showing total sales, order count and the cash vs Whish split, updating in real time.

☁

Google Drive backup

Back up the database to Google Drive and restore it on a new device. The last backup time is always visible.

HOW IT WORKS

From customer to closing report.

Select services

Tap the services the customer wants on the Sale tab.

Take payment

Choose Cash or Whish and the order is saved instantly.

Print the receipt

Show the receipt, then print it or save a PDF.

Review & back up

Check reports, export CSV, and back up to Drive.

BUILT WITH

Flutter, Drift & SQLite.

Orders, order items and services are stored in a local SQLite database through Drift, with reactive streams that keep reports and the services grid live.
